## 1. What went well

1. **Speed (Guy's first observation).** Every story started from a short spec with defaults for its open questions (7.1, 7.2, 7.3 specs merged by Guy without objection, defaults standing). No story waited on a decision.
2. **The UX pass came first (7.0).** Dialogs, panel cards and status bands were built once; 7.1, 7.2 and 7.3 assembled them instead of inventing screens.
3. **One report engine.** All PDFs share `report::pdf::Doc`: the width-metric fix (7.5) repaired the comparison, the review, the examen rapide and the study PDF at once.
4. **Reuse held.** The criblage (7.3 PR 2) reuses the examination whole (`QuickScreenSession`, `session_from_study` / `session_from_fetch`) and the 6.9 paced worker; no second fetch to open a row or create a study.
5. **The honesty doctrine held.** No currency invented for a criblage row without a study; « indisponible » vs « non examiné (limite d'usage) » kept distinct; the objective's pass / fail is kept off the criblage table (no ranking in disguise).
6. **Epic-6 gates honoured.** F1 (on-display walk), F2 (backlog triage), F4 (#94 staleness rail) and F5 (#98 sector source) were done before or during the epic.
7. **Live end-to-end check passed** with Guy's EODHD key (criblage fetch of ROG.SW, « Examiner un titre » → « Créer l'étude », 2016–2025 CHF, in-progress year dropped).

## 2. What was challenging

1. **Not everything was reviewed by the rules (Guy).** The 3-layer adversarial review (epic 6's safety net; it caught the 6.9 CRITICAL) ran on 7.4 only. 7.0–7.3 and 7.5 merged on tests + clippy + a headless walk.
2. **Visual defects are invisible to tests.** The PDFs measured text at 0.5 em per glyph since 5.6: column heads and notes crossed the grid while every test passed. Walks found them: six findings in the 7.0 walk, the PDF layouts in the 7.5 test, two more in its retest (a long reader note off the page; a text field widening its screen).
3. **Fixes introduced regressions.** « Moyenne » truncated in the study PDF and the search hint cut (« Rechercher un symb… »), both caught only by re-testing after the fix.
4. **Provider calls.** A real EODHD call ran headless in 7.3 PR 1 without Guy being asked (rule now recorded). A transient « connexion échouée » left no cause in the log. The failure notice says « les dernières données connues restent affichées » even in the examination, where nothing is displayed. EODHD's ROG.SW 2024 EPS (2,51 vs 17,6 in 2025) looks wrong and inflates the five-year P/E average.
5. **A record was inaccurate.** The 7.3 PR 1 verification noted NESN EPS −10,2 %: the report crate's demo study, not Guy's dossier (−4,4 %). Corrected in #232.
6. **Width at 1600 px.** The Études list rows (six fixed columns + five buttons) and the study's top band exceed a 1600 px window; the declared minimum is 720 px.

## 7. Key takeaways

1. **The speed came from the specs, not from skipping the review** — keep the spec-with-defaults habit, restore the review (G3).
2. **Tests check content, walks check geometry** — layout defects lived under green tests since 5.6; only on-screen checks find them, and a fix needs the same check (G4).
3. **Shared engines pay twice** — one `Doc` fix repaired four PDFs; one examination served the screen, the criblage and study creation.
4. **Provider calls are Guy's quota** — ask first (G5), and log enough to diagnose a failure (G7).
